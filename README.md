## 🕹️ About

Jupiter Moons: Mecha is a sci-fi roguelike deckbuilder built around tactical, turn-based mech combat. Choose a mech frame, customize its equipment, build a combat deck, and fight through a campaign while investigating an alien AI corruption spreading across the moons of Jupiter. This Windows build is an unfinished pre-release that surfaced ahead of schedule: raw, unstable, and not the final version.

For players who want tactical mech battles, roguelike progression, deep deckbuilding, and the rough edges of an early sci-fi prototype.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 **Mech Frames** — Choose between assault, stealth, and ranged mech frames with different combat identities.
- 🃏 **Deckbuilding Combat** — Turn weapons, shields, and equipment into a tactical combat deck.
- ⚔️ **Turn-Based Battles** — Predict enemy actions, extension cover, flank targets, and dismantle hostile machines.
- 🧩 **Modular Loadouts** — Upgrade frames and install equipment across individual mech body parts.
- 🔥 **System Damage** — Protect armor and health across separate body parts or lose access to attached cards.
- 🗺️ **Risky Campaign Routes** — Choose paths, weigh dangerous encounters, chase better rewards, and uncover the corrupted AI.
- 💥 **Experimental Arsenal** — Deploy lasers, plasma rifles, drones, mines, swords, hammers, cloaks, and stranger hardware.
